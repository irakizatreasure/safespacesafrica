# Content review and publication guide

All seeded lesson, FAQ, guide and source-candidate rows start as drafts. They are labeled AI-generated and pending authorized review. Draft body text and quiz answers are not served to public visitors.

## Suggested review sequence

1. **Editorial review:** check clarity, age suitability, plain language, tone, completeness and translations.
2. **Subject-matter review:** verify health, legal or digital-safety information against appropriate official sources and local requirements.
3. **Safeguarding review:** review child-facing and violence-related material for potential harm, disclosure pressure, victim-blaming and safe referrals.
4. **Approval:** the authorized reviewer records an approved state after review.
5. **Publication:** a CEO or President publishes the approved version. Public lesson text then appears and can be used by configured Cyizere retrieval.
6. **Scheduled review:** assign a real next-review date through the organization's content process; the current MVP does not yet automate review reminders.

The source candidate cards link to official WHO, UNESCO and UNICEF pages, but the links are not equivalent to a completed review. The help numbers on the Help page cite official Rwanda Government and Rwanda Investigation Bureau pages, with their check date shown. Reconfirm before launch.

## Editing

The content editor can edit title, summary and body, then save a draft or submit for review. A subject-matter reviewer can mark content approved. Only CEO or President can publish. Team profile writes are restricted to CEO and President at the API layer.

Keep identifying or survivor information out of educational content. Do not add fabricated staff biographies, partners, impact numbers, medical endorsements or publication dates.
